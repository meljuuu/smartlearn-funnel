SMARTLEARN DISCOVERY-CALL WEBSITE

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy describes information handled by SmartLearn's discovery-call website at smartlearn-funnel.polarisjumel.workers.dev. It covers this inquiry service, rather than every SmartLearn service or a separate website you choose to visit.

Information you provide

The form asks for your business name, business type, service area, team size, main administrative challenge, full name and email address. A phone number is optional. It also records your acknowledgment that SmartLearn may contact you about this inquiry. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive information about your customers or employees.

Why this information is used

Your information is used to review and respond to your discovery-call inquiry, discuss relevant administrative processes, arrange a call and keep a record of the inquiry and its status. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to a newsletter or SMS campaign. An inquiry does not book an appointment or commit you to paid services.

Where the inquiry goes

The website runs on Cloudflare. Submitted inquiries pass through Cloudflare and an authenticated Google Apps Script service into SmartLearn's restricted Google Workspace spreadsheet. The spreadsheet is not made public by this form. People with access authorized by SmartLearn can review the inquiry. If you email SmartLearn or choose a separate calendar booking page, that email or calendar provider also processes the information you provide there.

The next-steps page includes a Calendly booking frame. Loading that page connects your browser to Calendly, which receives connection information and may use its own cookies and storage. Details entered in that booking frame go to Calendly and the connected host calendar under their settings. The website does not put your inquiry name or email into the frame's URL. See Calendly privacy information and use the controls in its frame for its cookie choices.

Cloudflare and Google operate services internationally, so processing may occur outside Canada. Their own service terms and privacy information also apply: Cloudflare privacy information and Google privacy information.

Technical information and attribution

Cloudflare processes connection information such as your IP address to deliver the website. The inquiry service uses a keyed hash of the connection IP for temporary request limits; it does not put the raw IP address in the lead tracker. Random request identifiers and timestamps support duplicate prevention and recovery from an interrupted submission.

If your link includes campaign information, the inquiry can retain the five UTM fields (source, medium, campaign, content and term) and ad, ad-set and campaign identifiers. This website currently contains no Meta Pixel, advertising conversion sender or newsletter enrollment. Its font stylesheet is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the connection information needed to serve that request.

Your browser's session storage holds only opaque request identifiers, timestamps and submission-state flags for recovery; it does not store the form's contact details there. Session storage normally ends with the browser tab's session. The inquiry application does not set advertising cookies. External services may have their own storage practices.

Retention and safeguards

Inquiry records remain in SmartLearn's restricted tracker until reviewed and removed by the account owner. The current website does not apply an automatic deletion schedule. Minimal reply or stop-request records can remain to prevent unwanted contact. You can ask about access, correction, removal or stopping contact using the address below.

The website uses HTTPS, server-side validation, private signing credentials and restricted tracker access. These measures reduce risk; no internet transmission or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit the form, leave the optional phone field blank, or ask SmartLearn to stop contacting you about the inquiry. To request access to or correction of your submitted details, ask about removal, withdraw your request for contact, or raise a privacy concern, email info@smartlearn.ca. Include enough information to identify your inquiry without sending identity documents or sensitive credentials. Requests may require verification and consideration of any applicable retention obligations.

Changes to this policy

Updates to this website's handling of information will be reflected on this page with a revised date. A materially different use of inquiry information should be explained before that use begins.
