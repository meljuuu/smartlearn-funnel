# SmartLearn landing-page redesign

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This is a presentation update on the existing
`feat/durable-lead-capture` branch / draft PR #1, not a replacement funnel.

## Direction applied

- BDC and Molly Maid informed the readable, left-aligned text / single-image
  composition. Their photography, identities and copy were not reused.
- Boldly and PA Consulting informed generous space and stronger type hierarchy.
  SmartLearn keeps Inter and its own yellow, charcoal and warm cream palette.
- EOS and The Alternative Board informed starting with a recognizable business
  problem, then explaining the conversation. No claims or testimonials were borrowed.
- Milani, Ashton and 1-800-GOT-JUNK informed concrete service language and a clear
  inquiry action. No emergency-service urgency, ratings or guarantees were added.
- Consulting sites with elaborate animation, huge navigation systems or repeated
  promotional tiles were useful counterexamples. SmartLearn uses open ruled rows,
  a numbered discussion outline, a readable form and native expandable answers.

## Original visual and delivery

Created with the built-in image-generation tool (new image, opaque background).
The illustration is conceptual: paperwork, a weekly planner, an incoming envelope
and a completed follow-up. It does not depict SmartLearn software, clients or staff.
All actual explanations remain HTML. No decorative image captions are needed.

Production assets: `assets/workflow-480.jpg` (24,358 bytes),
`assets/workflow-800.jpg` (64,398 bytes), `assets/workflow-1200.jpg` (148,580 bytes).
Responsive `srcset` / `sizes`, explicit dimensions, high-priority hero loading,
and a considered mobile crop keep the workflow readable. A 120px derivative of
the supplied logo is `assets/logo-display.png` (11,120 bytes); the original
`assets/logo.png` remains byte-for-byte unchanged. Resizing used Windows' existing
System.Drawing encoder; no project dependency was added. Generated source is
preserved in `.review/hero-source.png` and the original generated-image directory.

Prompt:

> Create an original editorial illustration for SmartLearn, a practical business-process consultancy, for a website hero. Square composition, warm cream paper background (#f6f4ed), matte charcoal (#101317), off-white and restrained warm yellow (#faca21), very subtle printed-paper texture. A carefully arranged flat-lay working process: at left a small neat bundle of paperwork with simple charcoal rules, center a generous weekly planner grid with a few yellow blocks, at right an incoming envelope and a simple follow-up tick symbol. A single clear charcoal connecting route moves between the three objects, showing an inquiry becoming scheduled work and a recorded next action. Grounded objects aligned with the page, calm balanced composition with generous negative space and large clear shapes understandable on a phone. Thoughtful magazine illustration, tactile cut-paper and ink, understated shadows, no glossy 3D rendering. No words, letters, numbers, logos, people, invented software interfaces, screenshots, floating tilted card stacks, orbit lines, glows, robots, brains, sparkles, futuristic or circuit imagery. This is an illustration of administrative workflow, not a product dashboard. Keep all objects within the middle 85% so none need cropping.

## Funnel boundaries

The eight business categories, Other options, field names, required flags,
constraints, contact acknowledgment, honeypot, idempotency, timeout recovery,
server validation and signed Google capture are retained. Only the visible form
heading changes with its active step. Local review remains preview; deployment
retains the previously authorized live capture configuration. Email, follow-ups,
provider-event processing and advertising are not activated by this redesign.
