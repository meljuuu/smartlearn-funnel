What does the free call include?

We’ll discuss an administrative task, how you handle it today and where process improvements, digitization or suitable AI could help. A detailed assessment, on-site review or implementation project is separate work, with scope and fees agreed separately.

Will we have to replace our software?

The conversation starts with the tools you already use. A useful next step might be a clearer process, better use of an existing system or suitable new technology. There’s no assumption that you need new software.

Is this for my type of business?

We’re focusing on home-service businesses in Surrey and Langley: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, cleaning, roofing, renovation and property maintenance. If your work or service area is different, tell us about it in the form so we can discuss fit.

What happens after I send a request?

Once your inquiry has been saved, you’ll see next steps and the booking calendar. Choose an available time there to arrange your call. Sending the inquiry on its own does not book an appointment.
